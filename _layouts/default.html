About

Bridging hardware and software

I'm a Technical Program Manager based in Taipei, Taiwan, specialising in embedded software programs across ARM and x86 platforms. My work sits at the intersection of hardware engineering, firmware, and software teams — translating technical complexity into executable roadmaps.

At ASUS, I've managed NPI programs from engineering sample through mass production, coordinating ODM/ OEM partners, managing BOMs, and aligning cross-functional teams across BSP, driver, application, and QA workstreams.

Outside work I'm a freelance translator & interpreter (EN/ZH), an avid birdwatcher, photographer, and lifelong reader.
